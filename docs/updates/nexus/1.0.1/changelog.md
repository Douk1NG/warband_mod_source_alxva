# Version 1.0.1 — Kingdom Custom Troop Tree

## New Feature — Kingdom Custom Troop Tree

Redesigned the custom troop tree system. Choose from eight presets, preview
before confirming, and change your tree later.

### Import / Export — 12 Slots

- 8 seeded slots: the 6 native kingdoms plus Calradion and Falcon.
- 4 free slots for custom trees.
- With WSE, exported trees can be reused across campaigns and shared. Without
  WSE, the system works natively but exports stay in the current save.

### Per-Troop Editor

- Set Infantry, Cavalry, or Archers manually.
- Configure weapon modifiers and gender by branch.
- Use expanded proficiency values.

### Configurable Budget

Balanced, Boosted (1.5x), Cheater (3x), and Auto budgets. Children cannot be
weaker than their parent.

### Integration

Update party troops and garrisons on save. Castle reinforcements, town and
castle guards, and village recruits use the custom tree.

### Fixed in 1.0.1

- Fixed hand and hair textures on custom troops.
- Fixed companion inventory mass actions. Added a cheat to copy one hero's
  equipment to all companions.
- Fixed townspeople spawning on rooftops in Veluca.
- Fixed the Bathhouse Madam's prisoner count.

### Requirements

Mount & Blade: Warband. WSE is recommended but optional.
